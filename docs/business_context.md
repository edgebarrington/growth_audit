# Business Context

## Company Overview

### SynapseOS

**Mission:** Help knowledge workers eliminate busywork by transforming scattered information into actionable workflows.

SynapseOS is an AI-powered work operating system that connects tools such as Gmail, Slack, Notion, Google Drive, Calendar, and Zoom to automatically:

* Summarize meetings
* Extract action items
* Track project progress
* Generate reports
* Prioritize work
* Identify risks and blockers

The core value proposition is:

> Save 5+ hours per week without changing existing workflows.

# Growth History

## Months 1-3: Launch

Acquisition channels:

* Product Hunt
* LinkedIn
* Startup communities

Results:

* 2,000 signups
* 600 activated users

Strong initial demand driven by AI enthusiasm.

---

## Months 4-6: Rapid Growth

Results:

* 15,000 total users
* 4,500 activated users
* 1,200 paid users

Monthly growth averaged 20-25%.

Referral loops began contributing meaningfully.

---

## Months 7-9: Expansion

The company raised a seed round and grew from 5 to 14 employees.

Major launches:

* Meeting intelligence
* Workflow automation
* Project risk detection

Growth remained positive but began slowing.

---

## Months 10-12: Growth Slowdown

Leadership observed:

* Flat signup growth
* Lower activation rates
* Fewer team invitations
* Rising acquisition costs
* Declining free-to-paid conversion

Traffic remained healthy, but business performance weakened deeper in the funnel.

---

# Leadership Problem Statement

Three months ago, growth appeared predictable.

Today, revenue growth has slowed despite healthy traffic and signups.

Users continue trying the product, but fewer become active long-term users. Team expansion has slowed, referrals have weakened, and conversion rates are deteriorating.

Leadership needs to determine whether the slowdown is driven by:

* Product issues
* Onboarding friction
* Retention challenges
* Pricing problems
* Customer quality
* Market saturation

The expectation is to provide evidence-based recommendations rather than opinions.

# KPI Framework

## North Star Metric

### Weekly Active Workspaces (WAW)

A workspace is considered active if it completes at least one meaningful workflow during a week, such as:

* AI summary generation
* Workflow execution
* Report consumption
* Task completion

This metric best represents customer value creation.

# Assumptions for Dataset Design

The future synthetic datasets will be constructed around the following assumptions:

1. Traffic remains relatively stable.
2. Activation declines from 45% to 28%.
3. Users with only one integration rarely retain.
4. Recent acquisition campaigns bring lower-intent users.
5. Teams that invite 3+ members within 14 days convert at significantly higher rates.
6. Workflow automation adoption strongly predicts retention.
7. Referral-driven acquisition declines by approximately 40%.
8. Leadership has optimized for signups rather than activation.

These assumptions create a realistic scenario where growth slowdown is driven by multiple interconnected factors rather than a single obvious cause.
